Data Deletion

AdPilot data deletion instructions

Effective date: 2026-07-29 · Last updated: 2026-07-29

These deletion instructions are a technical draft for review. The operator should confirm the exact deletion email address, verification steps, and completion period before production launch.

How to disconnect Meta

If you want to stop AdPilot from accessing your Meta advertising data, you should disconnect the Meta integration from the AdPilot application settings or from your Meta account permissions. The exact UI path may vary by product version.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your AdPilot account and associated data, send a deletion request to the privacy or data-protection email that the operator should publish before launch. Please include the email address associated with the AdPilot account, a brief description of the request, and any information needed to verify that you are authorized to act on the account.

What will be deleted

A deletion request will aim to remove account information, Meta connection details, stored OAuth-related material, campaign and ad metadata, performance records, settings, alert preferences, and related operational records that are not required for ongoing legal or security purposes.

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, backup-recovery, or dispute resolution. Retention will generally be limited to the minimum period needed and should be reviewed as part of the final production deployment.

Completion period

The operator should define the expected deletion-completion period before launch. This draft recommends a completion window of 30 days after verification of the request, unless applicable law requires a shorter or longer period.

Confirmation

Once the request is reviewed and verified, the operator should send a confirmation message to the requester confirming that the request has been received and that deletion is being processed.

Meta Business Integrations

To remove AdPilot access from Meta, you may also revoke the app’s permissions through Facebook Business Integrations or the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking Meta access and deleting your AdPilot account are related but separate actions.

This page is intended to describe the current product behavior accurately. If you need a finalized deletion workflow for launch, obtain legal review and confirm the operational process before deployment.
