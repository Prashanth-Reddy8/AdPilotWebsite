Privacy Policy

AdPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-29 · Last updated: 2026-07-29

This policy is a technical draft for review. The operator should obtain qualified legal review before production launch and confirm the business name, address, contacts, and hosting providers used in production.

1. Who operates AdPilot

AdPilot is operated by the business entity responsible for the AdPilot service. Because the legal business name, registered address, and jurisdiction have not yet been finalized, those details should be inserted before launch. The operator should also provide a public support email and a privacy/data protection contact email.

2. What information AdPilot collects

AdPilot processes information that users provide when creating an account and information that is needed to connect the service to Meta advertising accounts. This may include:

  • User account and authentication data, such as email address, account identifiers, and hashed or encrypted credentials or tokens.
  • Meta account identifiers and ad-account identifiers associated with a user’s authorized Meta connection.
  • Campaign, ad set, ad, and creative metadata such as names, IDs, and account structure information.
  • Advertising performance metrics such as spend, revenue, impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, frequency, purchases, CPA, and ROAS.
  • Product settings, alert preferences, Slack notification preferences, and related configuration data.
  • Technical and operational information such as logs, error details, request metadata, and security-relevant diagnostics.

3. Why AdPilot processes this information

AdPilot uses the information described above to provide the current product experience, including:

  • Authenticating users and maintaining account access.
  • Connecting to Meta advertising accounts through a read-only integration.
  • Importing and normalizing advertising performance data for analysis and recommendations.
  • Generating recommendations and alerts based on the imported data.
  • Supporting Slack notifications when enabled by the user.
  • Maintaining security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

4. Meta data and the current product

AdPilot reads Meta advertising data for the purpose of monitoring and analyzing creative performance. The current Meta integration is read-only. AdPilot requests the Meta ads_read permission and does not automatically pause, edit, create, or delete campaigns or ads. The service may present recommendations and alerts, but the user remains responsible for any advertising decisions.

5. OAuth tokens and protection

When a user authorizes AdPilot to access Meta advertising data, AdPilot stores OAuth-related access material in a protected form. The application uses encryption for stored access tokens and applies access controls to limit exposure. Security measures reduce the risk of unauthorized access, but no system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

6. Sharing and service providers

AdPilot may share the information described above with service providers that help operate the product, including hosting, database, and notification infrastructure providers. If Slack notifications are enabled, AdPilot may transmit alert content to Slack through the configured integration. AdPilot does not sell Meta data or advertising data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Retention

AdPilot retains data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data, subject to any retention that is required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or backup-recovery purposes.

8. Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data. Requests should be sent to the privacy contact email that the operator should publish before launch. The data-deletion page at /data-deletion explains the deletion process in more detail.

9. International data transfers

AdPilot may process information in jurisdictions where the service providers and hosting infrastructure operate. Where required by applicable law, the operator should implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Children’s privacy

AdPilot is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. If the operator becomes aware that a child has provided personal information without proper authorization, it should delete that information promptly.

11. Updates to this policy

AdPilot may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and the “Last updated” notice on this page.

12. Meta Platform Terms

The Meta integration and related data access are subject to Meta’s Platform Terms and applicable policies. AdPilot does not change or override those terms.

This policy is intended to describe the current product behavior accurately. If you need a finalized legal version for launch, the business should obtain professional legal review before deployment.
